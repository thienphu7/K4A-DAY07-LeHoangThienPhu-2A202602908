# Limits on borrowing

## Standard limits

General Collection loans are unlimited in number. Their loan period is at least 7 days and may extend to 365 days, subject to recall conditions.

Only two Short Loan items may be borrowed at once. Each Short Loan lasts 3 hours.

## Overnight Short Loans

A Short Loan may be kept overnight only when it is borrowed within 3 hours of the information desk closing. It must be returned to the same location within the first hour after that desk opens the following day.

## Recalls

Every item is subject to recall. When another client recalls an item, the current borrower receives an email and has 7 days to return it before it becomes overdue.
