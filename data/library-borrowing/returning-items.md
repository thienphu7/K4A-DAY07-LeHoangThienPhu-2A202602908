# Returning items

## Return chutes

Every University of Sydney library location has a return chute. The chutes at Fisher, SciTech, and Camden Commons operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Most items may be returned through a chute at any University of Sydney library, regardless of where they were borrowed. A Short Loan item is an exception: it must go back to the location from which it was borrowed.

## Libcabinets

Material borrowed from a Libcabinet must be returned to that same machine. The borrower scans their card, opens the door, places the material on any shelf, and closes the door. Closing the door removes the item from the Library account.

## Postal returns

Registered or express post is recommended so a postal return can be tracked. The Library removes the item from the borrower's account after receiving and checking it in.

Postal address: University of Sydney Library, University of Sydney, NSW 2006, Australia.
