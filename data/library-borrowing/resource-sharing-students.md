# Resource sharing for eligible students

## Eligibility and allowance

The Resource Sharing service is available to postgraduate students and honours students. Eligible students may borrow up to 100 items from other institutions in each calendar year. Requests remain subject to Australian copyright law, which may limit how much of a book or journal can be supplied.

## Request process

The student signs in to the Library catalogue and searches the University collection. If the required item is absent, the student selects the option to include items shared by other libraries, chooses an available physical or digital option, and submits the request. Physical items are delivered to a selected University of Sydney Library; digital chapters or articles are sent by email.

## Timing and renewals

An external-library loan normally lasts at least 3 weeks, although the supplying institution sets the actual period. These loans generally cannot be renewed. Items from Australian libraries usually arrive in 7 to 10 business days; international items can take up to 4 weeks.
