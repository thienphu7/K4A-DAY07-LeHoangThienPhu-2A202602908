# Requesting items

## Transfers and recalls

A General Collection item can be transferred between the Conservatorium Library and the Camperdown/Darlington campus. Items already on the shelves at Fisher, Law, SciTech, or the Susan Wakil Health Building cannot be transferred between those locations and must be borrowed in person.

A borrower may recall a General Collection item whose status is on loan, on the hold shelf, or in transit. The current borrower is then told to return it within 7 days. After return, the requester receives an email and the item is placed on the hold shelf.

## Library Store requests

An item held in the off-site Library Store may be requested as a physical item sent to a Library, or as a digital copy when it is a journal article or book chapter. Digital copies usually arrive by email in 24 to 48 hours. Physical items are normally ready at Fisher Library within 2 to 3 days; other pickup locations require more time.

## Collection and digitisation

An item remains on the hold shelf for 7 days after the availability email is sent.

To request digitisation, the user signs in to the catalogue, selects the journal and location, chooses “Request a digital copy,” completes the form and copyright acknowledgement, and submits the request. The finished copy is delivered by email.
