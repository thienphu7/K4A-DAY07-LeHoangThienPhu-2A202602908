# Resource sharing for staff

## Eligibility and allowance

University of Sydney staff are eligible for Resource Sharing. Staff may borrow up to 100 items from other institutions in each calendar year. Requests are subject to Australian copyright law, which may limit the portion of a book or journal that can be supplied.

## Request process

The staff member signs in to the Library catalogue and searches the University collection. If the item is unavailable, the search can be expanded to items shared by other institutions. A physical copy can be collected from a selected University of Sydney Library, while a requested digital chapter or article is delivered by email.

## Timing and renewals

The usual minimum loan period is 3 weeks, but the lending institution may set a different period. External-library loans generally cannot be renewed. Requests from Australian libraries usually arrive in 7 to 10 business days, whereas international requests may take up to 4 weeks.
