# Borrowing terms and conditions

## General Collection loans

The guaranteed borrowing period is 7 days. A borrower may keep an item for as long as 365 days only while all of these conditions remain true:

- no other client has recalled the item;
- the borrower's University of Sydney enrolment or membership remains current; and
- the borrowing record has no fines or blocks.

General Collection loans cannot be renewed and may be recalled, which shortens the original loan period.

## Overdue, lost, or damaged items

An item is marked lost when it is at least 32 days overdue, is returned damaged, or cannot be located by the borrower. Until a lost item is returned or paid for, the Library blocks further borrowing and charges a $100 administration fee. Replacement copies are not accepted. If the matter is unresolved for 3 months, academic sanctions may be applied, including restrictions on enrolment or graduation.

A lost or non-repairable laptop from a vending machine attracts a $1,250 replacement fee. The laptop repair fee is $50.

Administrative fees can be paid through the Library account.
